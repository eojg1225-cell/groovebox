Bass TIMBRE Sweep

The BASS track's TIMBRE across its five sounds, on the real engine. Between GROWL and FM 2:3 the FM ratios change; compare the app now (the carrier jumps halfway) with a stepped version that passes through 2 : 3 on the way. Keys 1-2 switch the rule, S starts the auto sweep, Space plays.

126 BPM · A minor

What to listen for

Run the auto sweep (8 bars each way) on NOW, then on STEPPED. On NOW the tone changes once, in the middle. On STEPPED it should change in two smaller moves. Below GROWL nothing differs: the other sounds all share the same ratios. Tell Claude which rule to keep.
