Bass TIMBRE Sweep

The BASS track's TIMBRE across its five sounds, on the real engine: the proposed BASS with FM LVL separated from INTENSITY, with and without a little FM on the first four sounds. INTENSITY starts at 0. Keys 1-3 switch, S starts the auto sweep, Space plays.

126 BPM · A minor

What to listen for

With INTENSITY at 0, run the auto sweep (16 bars each way) on 1, then on 2. On 1 the first part of the knob should sound much as today's sub, growl, acid and solid; on 2 they gain a little FM colour, changing at the 1/3 and 2/3 points. FM 2:3 keeps its FM at INT 0 on both (on 3, the app now, INT 0 takes it away). Tell Claude 1 or 2.
