TIMBRE Sound Test

Your project, with the chosen track's sound driven only by TIMBRE and INT. PAD: a MIN7 chord held for 16 steps (one bar), then struck again. Press Play, sweep TIMBRE and INT slowly and switch engines (keys A-D) to compare them at the same position. C and D are the two-stack pad; on both, INT tops out at FM level 92. Gentle INT lowers how far INT pushes the modulation on every engine.

How TIMBRE moves between sounds

Drag a knob up or down, scroll on it, or use the arrow keys. Tap a sound name to jump TIMBRE to it. Space plays and stops. INT works the same way in every option. The sounds use each track's default envelope and width settings, not your SOUND page adjustments.
